About the center

Rigorous research, grounded in human needs.

Axiom is a university research center working across computer science, design, and the sciences to create AI that is reliable, understandable, and broadly useful.

Our mission

We pursue foundational and applied research that improves how intelligent systems learn, reason, and collaborate with people. We value work that is technically ambitious, empirically careful, openly communicated, and attentive to its wider consequences.

How we work

Research happens in small cross-disciplinary teams. Faculty and students share ownership of questions, methods, software, and communication. We collaborate with public-interest organizations, scientific institutions, and industry partners when those relationships strengthen the research and its real-world relevance.

Our commitments

Open scholarship

We share papers, code, data, and evaluation methods whenever responsible and feasible.

Responsible practice

We examine who benefits, who carries risk, and how systems behave outside the lab.

Student development

We create meaningful research roles with close mentorship and room for intellectual independence.

Plural perspectives

We bring technical, social, and domain expertise into the same conversation.
