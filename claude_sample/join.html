Get involved

Join us

We welcome students, visitors, collaborators and partners. Below are the ways to get involved.

Prospective PhD students

Apply through the Department of Computer Science graduate program and name the faculty you want to work with in your statement. The deadline is usually December 15.

Graduate admissions →

Master's & undergraduate students

Come to the Reading Club, take our AI courses, then email a faculty member with your CV, transcript and a few sentences about what interests you.

Reading Club →

Visiting researchers

We host visiting students and scholars for 3–12 months. Contact the faculty member you would like to work with, and copy the Center Coordinator.

Industry & public-sector partners

We collaborate through sponsored research, joint projects, internships and an annual partners day.
