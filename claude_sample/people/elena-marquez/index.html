Professor · Director, Center for AI Research

Elena Márquez

Elena Márquez is a Professor of Computer Science and the founding Director of the Center for AI Research. Her group studies how language models reason and how to make their outputs faithful and verifiable. The group also builds language technology for under-resourced languages. Before joining [University Name] she was a postdoctoral researcher at [Institution]. She received her PhD from [University].
