About

About the Center

The Center for AI Research (CAIR) is an interdisciplinary research center within the Department of Computer Science at [University Name]. We study the foundations and applications of artificial intelligence, and we care about how it is built and used.

Mission

Our mission is to advance the science of artificial intelligence, to educate researchers and engineers who will shape the field, and to make sure the systems we build are reliable, fair and beneficial to society.

What we do

  • Fundamental research in machine learning, language, vision, robotics and responsible AI, published at leading venues.
  • Education: graduate and undergraduate courses, supervised research, a weekly seminar and a student-led reading club.
  • Collaboration with other departments, such as medicine, linguistics and the social sciences, and with industry and government partners.
  • Open science: we release code, datasets and models whenever possible.
