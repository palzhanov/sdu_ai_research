Research

Research overview

Our work spans the full range from theory to deployed systems. Projects are often shared across groups. Below are our main research themes and some active projects.

Language & Reasoning

We study how language models represent knowledge and carry out reasoning, and how to make their outputs faithful and verifiable. We also build NLP resources for low-resource and regional languages.

LLMsReasoningMultilingual NLPEvaluation

Vision & Embodied AI

We develop models that perceive and act in the physical world, including 3D scene understanding, video models, and learning-based control for manipulation and navigation.

Computer visionRoboticsSim-to-real3D

Learning Theory & Optimization

We work on why deep networks generalize, on efficient and stable training methods, and on learning when data and labels are scarce.

GeneralizationOptimizationEfficient ML

Responsible & Trustworthy AI

We develop methods for auditing fairness, explaining model behavior and keeping models robust under distribution shift. We work with social scientists and policymakers on how AI is governed.

FairnessInterpretabilityRobustnessPolicy
